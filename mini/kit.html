Use the desktop version of Chrome, Edge or Brave. Stop the sequencer (Play off) before sending. First preview the kit (step 3; it is lost when you turn CLAVE off). If you like it, save it to a user slot (step 4) and it stays there even when you turn CLAVE off. Nothing is installed: the samples go to CLAVE's memory.

Upload Sound Kits

0 Prepare your samples.

  • Mono WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16 or 24 bit.
  • Up to 3 s per sound and about 14 s in total for the 8 sounds of the kit. Shorter is better.
  • No silence at the start, and a short fade-out at the end.
  • Normalized close to 0 dB: this page doesn't change the volume.
  • Samples are mono; the output is stereo. Each channel has its own pan.

1 Connect CLAVE through USB-C J1 (no MIDI device plugged in) and press connect usb.

Not connected.

2 Choose a sound for each channel. Empty channels keep their current sound.

    Counts the files you choose (each one is cut at 3 s). Channels you don't replace keep their current sound, and that sound also takes space.

    Guide: in the factory kits the order is usually 1 kick, 2 snare, 3 hi-hat, 4 tom, 5 conga, 6 cowbell, 7 shaker, 8 clap.

    3 Preview the kit. It goes to temporary memory and is lost when you turn CLAVE off.

    4 If you like it, save it to a user slot. It stays when you turn CLAVE off.

    On CLAVE, choose the kit with shift + preset. On the ring, the factory kits come first (green LEDs) and your slots right after them (purple LEDs); empty slots stay off.
