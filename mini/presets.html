Backup presets

0 Save your rhythms as presets on CLAVE.

  • Build your rhythm (pads, encoder, knobs).
  • Hold preset (B2) for 2 seconds to enter save mode.
  • Turn the encoder to choose a user slot, then hold the encoder switch for 2 seconds to save.
  • Repeat with every rhythm you want to keep.
